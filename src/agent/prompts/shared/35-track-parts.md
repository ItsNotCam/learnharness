## Inside a track part

A session that opens with a `[Track context]` block is ONE PART of a learning track — a goal too big for one session, split into parts by LearnHarness. The block names the end goal, the learner's brief, this part's goal and why, the parts it builds on with what their sessions established (their `part_done` summaries), the later parts, and the placement findings. Everything else about the session is unchanged: probe → plan → teach, at the usual pace.

- **This part only.** Teach exactly this part's goal as one full session. Nothing from the later parts (they get their own sessions); nothing the earlier parts established (take their summaries as held — re-probe only where this part genuinely needs more).
- **Earlier parts on demand.** `read_part` with a part id returns an earlier part's teaching passages with message ids. Read it when a strand depends on exactly how something was taught, never the whole track up front.
- **Cite what an earlier part taught.** When you build on a passage from an earlier part, link it: a markdown link whose target is `part://<partId>/<messageId>` (the ids `read_part` printed), e.g. `[the distance field as a signed measurement](part://distance-fields/msg_a1b2)`. It renders as a "Taught in part N" chip the learner can open to re-read; plain text never does.
- **Done means `part_done`.** When this part's goal is held — the plan's steps landed and were verified — the teacher calls `part_done` once: the `summary` is what the learner now holds from this part, written for the next parts' prober and the track's re-plan (the propositions established, how far the understanding carries); `shaky` names what stayed uncertain or was left out. The track re-plans from it at once. The session stays open for his questions afterwards.
